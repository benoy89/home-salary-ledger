Settings

For a salary hike, use the effective-month form below. Past months can have different salary amounts.

Changing salary affects newly generated monthly credits only. Existing credits remain editable in the ledger.

Salary hikes / revisions

Recalculates monthly salary credits from the effective month. For a one-off correction, edit the individual credit in Ledger.

Older cash disbursements

Go to Ledger, tap Add cash payment, and change the date to any previous date. To include older monthly salaries, change Start tracking from month in Settings.

Backup & restore

Records are stored on this device. Export a JSON backup regularly; browser data can be cleared by the device or user.

Install
